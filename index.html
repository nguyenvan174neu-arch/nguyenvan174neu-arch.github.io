About

I am a PhD Candidate in Finance at the Wellington School of Business and Government, Victoria University of Wellington.

My research focuses on climate finance, carbon markets, climate-related financial disclosure, sustainable finance, and financial regulation.

Research

Mandatory Climate-related Disclosure and the Cost of Equity

Examining the effects of mandatory climate-related financial disclosure regulation on firms' cost of equity across New Zealand, the United Kingdom, and Japan.

Carbon Price Shocks and Stock Returns

Investigating how unexpected changes in carbon prices affect stock market returns under the New Zealand Emissions Trading Scheme.

Carbon Prices, Firm Performance and Long-term Investment

Studying the relationship between carbon price shocks, firm financial performance, investment, and innovation.
